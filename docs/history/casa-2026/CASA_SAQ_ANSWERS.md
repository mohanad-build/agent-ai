were ever unset or blank (`'' !== ''` is false). Fail-closed guard added.

## Environment additions

`TOKEN_ENCRYPTION_KEY` (32-byte, **backed up outside Railway — losing it means
every agent re-authorizes Google**), `DASHBOARD_MFA_PHONE` (E.164 operator
number, required or login dead-ends at code entry).

Cloudflare: Minimum TLS Version raised from 1.0 (default) to **TLS 1.2**.
Deliberately not 1.3 — Twilio's inbound webhook connections are not under our
control and a 1.3 floor risks silently breaking reply detection at the
handshake. This took SSL Labs from **B to A**.

## Determinations

29 IMPLEMENTED, 12 NOT APPLICABLE, 5 platform-evidenced (TLS ×4, DNS ×1),
2 ACCEPTED RISK:

- **`3.1.4` IDOR** — single shared operator password, no per-agent
  authorization boundary. Intentional: agents are data records, not user
  accounts. Mitigations are MFA, rate limiting, Secure/HttpOnly cookies with
  12h lifetime, generic errors, and validated path construction. Framed
  honestly rather than claiming a boundary that doesn't exist.
- **`6.1.1` npm audit** — 4 moderate `googleapis`-chain advisories remaining,
  resolvable only via a breaking major bump. Not reachable through this
  app's usage of the library; deferred to the next maintenance cycle.

These two are the rows a tester is most likely to challenge.

4. `WEBHOOK_SKIP_SIGNATURE_CHECK` still present in the production environment.
   Host-gated to localhost/ngrok so almost certainly inert, but unnecessary.

   since that alters the URL reconstruction Twilio signature validation
   depends on — change one thing at a time and re-test inbound SMS after.
